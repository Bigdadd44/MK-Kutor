About MK Kutor

MK Kutor is a boutique tailoring house dedicated to honoring craftsmanship, detail, and the stories behind every garment. With years of experience in custom design, alterations, and traditional sewing techniques, we bring precision and care to each stitch.

From bespoke dresses to intricate embroidery, our services blend timeless techniques with modern aesthetics.
